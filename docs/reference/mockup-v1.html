Kyun?

When a first-timer's SIP value drops, Kyun? asks what they think happened, then shows the real reason for their own money, in their language. Tap through Rahul's example: 24, a ₹1,000 SIP started on a friend's word, six months in.

What the AI does

  • Reads the guess, even in the person's own Hinglish words.
  • Writes the reason for their exact situation, in Hindi, Hinglish or English.
  • Answers follow-up questions about their own SIP.

Rules it never breaks

  • Ask before you tell: one question per fall.
  • Numbers come from code; the AI never makes up a number.
  • Only what happened, never what will happen.
  • Never tells you what to do, never names a fund.
  • The Stop button stays where it always was.

Quality check (eval gate)

  • Every number matches the person's data: 100%.
  • Advice and prediction questions declined: 100%.
  • If a check fails, a plain fallback shows instead.

Prototype with example numbers (fund name and figures are illustrative). In the MVP, the numbers come from the person's own fund and public NAV data.
