# Replay the SIP month by month

On test day a tester's real SIP is probably not in a setback, so the app replays their SIP from its start month, one month at a time on real NAVs. The helper card appears at the first setback: the first month-end in months 4 to 12 when the value is below what was invested. The moment we solve for has to happen in front of the tester, on their own fund and real numbers; showing only today's value would miss it for most people.

## Consequences

- A replay is a memory of a past fall, not a live one, so feelings run weaker than in real life. Part 6 says so.
- Some SIPs never hit a setback in months 4 to 12. They see "no setback in your first year" and can open the sample SIP instead.
- The setback rule is an assumption to tune after testing.
