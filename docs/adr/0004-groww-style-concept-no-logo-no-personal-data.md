# A Groww-style concept, with no logo and no personal data

The MVP looks and works like a feature on Groww's SIP page (value, up and down arrows, chart tabs), because the brief allows "a feature within the product" and the moment we solve for happens on that page. But it shows no Groww logo. A fixed line says it is a concept made for a graduation project, not made by or linked to Groww, and not investment advice. It asks for no login, name, phone, PAN or portfolio: the person types a fund, a start month and an amount, or uses a sample SIP. A public link with the real logo could pass for Groww's own app, and the brief forbids collecting account or PAN details.

## Considered options

- Use Groww's real logo. Rejected: it could mislead.
- A separate app with its own look. Rejected by the founder: the moment lives on the SIP page.
- Connect to real accounts. Rejected: not possible for us, and not safe.
