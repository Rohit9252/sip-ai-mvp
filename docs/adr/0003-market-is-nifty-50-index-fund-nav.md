# "The market" is a Nifty 50 index fund's NAV

"The whole market" is the NAV of UTI Nifty 50 Index Fund, Direct, Growth (AMFI scheme 120716), taken over the same dates as the person's fund. It comes free from the same public AMFI source as the person's fund, on the same calendar, with no index data licence. We accept that it differs a little from the index itself (fees and tracking error) and that it covers large companies only.

## Consequences

For a small-cap fund, part of a fall may be "small companies fell" rather than "my fund did badly". The reason says "the Nifty 50" plainly and does not claim more. A comparison with the fund's own category is a later step.
