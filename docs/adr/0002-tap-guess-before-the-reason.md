# A tapped guess before the reason

Before showing the reason, the helper card asks "what do you think happened?" with four tap choices: my fund, the whole market, don't know, not worth it. There is no free text. Our lead root cause is that first-timers misread a fall; asking first brings the misread into the open, lets the reason start from it, and gives us the input metric "falls read correctly". Taps keep it simple for a first-timer, leave the AI nothing to misread, and keep it to one question per setback (the guardrail is one question or fewer).

## Considered options

- Free text read by the AI. Dropped by the founder on 2 Nov: simpler is better for this user.
- No question, straight to the reason. Rejected: we would lose the misread and the metric.
